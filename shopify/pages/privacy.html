This policy explains what information Terrane collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. Questions? Email barcus@terranemaps.com.

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us — your name and email when you request a map, place an order, or write to us.
  • Design details — the place, coordinates, framing, and any legend text you create in the studio, so we can build your map.
  • Order and payment information — processed securely by our store and payment providers (such as Shopify and PayPal). We do not store your full card details ourselves.
  • Basic usage data — we use privacy-respecting, first-party analytics to understand how the site is used. We do not run third-party advertising trackers.

How we use it

To build and ship your map, send your proof, process payment, respond to your messages, and improve the site. We use your email to send order and proof updates, and only send other messages if you ask us to.

What we don’t do

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we never reproduce or resell your map design. Your design is an edition of one.

Sharing

We share information only with the providers that help us run the store — payment processing, order fulfillment, and shipping — and only as needed to complete your order, or where required by law.

Cookies

We use essential cookies to run the store and keep your cart and session working, plus first-party analytics to understand site usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Your choices

You can ask us what personal information we hold, correct it, or delete it. Email barcus@terranemaps.com and we will take care of it.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; the current version always lives on this page.
