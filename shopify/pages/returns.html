Every Terrane map is built from scratch for one specific place, as an edition of one. That shapes how returns work.

Made-to-order items

Because each map is custom-made for a single place and printed only once, we do not accept returns or exchanges on completed orders.

If it arrives damaged or defective

We stand behind every piece. If your map arrives damaged, or there is a defect in how it was printed or finished, we will remake or refund it, free, within 30 days of delivery.

How to reach us

Email barcus@terranemaps.com within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo of the issue. We will sort out a remake or refund right away — no runaround.

Cancellations and changes

You can change or cancel your order any time before you approve your proof. Once you approve the proof, your map goes into production and can no longer be changed or cancelled.

The proof protects you

The proof is your chance to get every detail right. We email you the final render and print nothing until you approve it, so you always see exactly what you are getting first.
