What exactly is a Terrane map?

A made-to-order, 3D-printed relief model of a real place — an 8" × 8" square you can hang on the wall and run a thumb across. The terrain is raised in true scale, built from real elevation and street data, not a flat print of a map.

Where does the data come from?

Elevation from the USGS 3D Elevation Program (aerial lidar), water and shorelines from NOAA, and streets and place names from OpenStreetMap. We resolve your place to exact coordinates and build the geometry from those measurements.

How accurate is it?

Coordinates are resolved and recorded to four decimal places, and the scale is stated as a true ratio computed from your frame and the ground it covers. The shape of the land is measured, not stylized.

What is the proof, and when do I see it?

After you frame your place in the studio, we build the final render from survey data and email you a proof to review. Nothing is printed until you approve it — no surprise mountains.

Can I put a name, date, or message on it?

Yes. The second line of the legend is yours — a name, a date, the reason. We print it and never ask.

What size is it, and how much?

One size: an 8" × 8" square, $249, made to order. Simple on purpose.

What is it made of?

It is 3D printed layer by layer in durable PLA, finished by hand, and mounted ready to hang.

How long does it take?

Because each map is built for one place, one time, it typically ships within one week of proof approval. You will get tracking when it is on the way.

Do you ship internationally?

We currently ship within the United States with free, tracked shipping. International shipping is not offered yet — email us and we will let you know when it is.

Can I return it?

Because every map is made to order for a single place, we do not accept returns or exchanges. If your map arrives damaged or defective, we will remake or refund it, free, within 30 days of delivery. See Returns.

Do you reuse or resell my design?

Never. Your design is an edition of one — printed a single time, for you, and never reproduced or resold.

What if I can’t find my place?

Search an address or city, enter coordinates, or upload a GPX file. If you can point to it, we can build it — and if you get stuck, email barcus@terranemaps.com and we will help you frame it.
